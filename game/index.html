PUERTO AZAHAR · 1989

You are Nico Ferran, a dock runner with empty pockets and a borrowed name. The port city of Puerto Azahar runs on two things: fish by day and contraband by night. Lucia Ferrer, who owns half the cafés on the marina, is offering you work — small jobs at first. How far you climb is up to you. Just watch the stars: in Azahar, the police count them for you.
